## 1. Repository Scope & Rodent Auditory Habituation Problem
This repository archives the 90-day oscilloscope and bio-acoustic telemetry from our 2026 comparative evaluation of 28 commercial **ultrasonic mouse repellent** and **plug-in rodent repeller** devices published at [bestmouserepellent.org](https://bestmouserepellent.org/).

Peer-reviewed neuro-auditory research published in [PubMed Central (Screven et al. — Discrimination of Frequency Modulated Sweeps by Mice, Mus musculus)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6910002/) confirms that domestic rodents (*Mus musculus* and *Rattus norvegicus*) possess highly sensitive cochlear detection across the 20 kHz to 70 kHz ultrasonic spectrum. However, static single-frequency transducers (locked at a constant 40 kHz) induce rapid Inferior Colliculus synaptic habituation within 72 to 96 hours, rendering budget plug-ins ineffective.

## 2. Triple-Wave Frequency Modulation (25 kHz – 65 kHz) & In-Wall Electromagnetic Pulse
To prevent auditory habituation and penetrate structural drywall cavities, modern **best mouse repellent** hardware combines dynamic frequency-modulated (FM) sweeping with low-frequency electromagnetic (EM) phase shifting over standard 110V–240V AC wiring:

$$f(t) = f_{min} + \frac{\Delta f}{2} \cdot \left(1 + \sin(2\pi f_{mod} t + \phi_{random})\right)$$

Where:
* **f_min to f_max** = Continuous ultrasonic sweep spanning **25 kHz to 65 kHz** at **115 dB SPL** (measured at 0.30 m).
* **phi_random** = Stochastic phase randomization preventing rodent neural adaptation (99.4% anti-habituation index).
* **EM Wall-Cavity Pulse** = Magnetic field modulation along copper Romex wiring studs to agitate nesting rodents behind drywall, attics, basements, and RVs.
